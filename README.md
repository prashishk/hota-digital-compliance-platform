# HOTA Digital Compliance Platform

> **Enterprise digital-transformation case study** — designing a governed digital workflow for hospital registration, regulatory compliance assessment, specialist review and certification.

## Executive Summary

This case study demonstrates how a complex, document-heavy regulatory process can be transformed into a **structured digital compliance workflow** with explicit eligibility checks, evidence, specialist review, decision gates and auditability.

The product model is intentionally designed around a simple principle:

> **Make compliance evidence structured, reviewable and traceable — without removing accountable human judgement.**

## Transformation

**Manual / fragmented model**

`Registration → Document Collection → Specialist Checking → Clarification → Decision → Certification`

**Target digital model**

`Structured Registration → Eligibility Rules → Evidence Workspace → Specialist Review → Exceptions → Approval → Certification → Audit`

## Confidentiality & Scope

This is a **redacted / portfolio representation** of digital-transformation work. It intentionally excludes patient information, hospital credentials, confidential source code, personally identifiable information and restricted government material.

Regulatory requirements should always be validated against the applicable current law, rules, authority guidance and operating procedures before production implementation. Any future-state capability labelled **proposed** is a design/POC concept, not a claim of production deployment.
