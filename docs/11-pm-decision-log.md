# 11 — PM Decision Log

| Decision | Why | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| State-based workflow | makes ownership and status explicit | requires clear state design |
| Deterministic eligibility rules | regulatory decisions need consistency | rules require governance |
| Evidence-linked decisions | strengthens auditability | stronger data modelling required |
| Exception workflow | prevents offline workarounds | adds controlled process overhead |
| AI as assistive | enables productivity with lower risk | limits autonomous automation |

## PM Principle

For regulated digital products, **delivery speed is subordinate to correctness, traceability and operational control**.
