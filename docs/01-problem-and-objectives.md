# 01 — Problem & Objectives

## Problem

Regulatory certification can involve multiple documents, eligibility checks, specialist reviews, clarifications and approvals. When those activities are fragmented, teams lose visibility into **what is complete, what is missing, who must act and why a decision was made**.

## Transformation Objective

Create a controlled digital workflow that makes each compliance case:

- structured
- evidence-backed
- role-aware
- reviewable
- traceable

## Objectives

1. Digitise registration and case intake.
2. Standardise eligibility and evidence collection.
3. Route cases to the appropriate specialist reviewers.
4. Track clarifications and exceptions.
5. Preserve decision history and certification evidence.
6. Establish measurable service and compliance KPIs.

## Success Measures

- first-pass completeness
- average review turnaround
- clarification / rework rate
- cases ageing beyond SLA
- % decisions with complete evidence
- certification cycle time
