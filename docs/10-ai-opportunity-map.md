# 10 — AI Opportunity Map

AI can reduce review effort without becoming the regulatory decision-maker.

| Opportunity | AI contribution | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence classification | classify documents / evidence | reviewer confirms |
| Completeness review | flag potentially missing evidence | user validates |
| Policy retrieval | surface relevant approved guidance | source must be visible |
| Case summarisation | summarise history and findings | reviewer checks source |
| Clarification drafting | draft questions | reviewer edits/sends |
| Workload intelligence | identify ageing / patterns | manager decides |

## Guardrails

- no autonomous certification
- no autonomous rejection
- source-grounded outputs
- human confirmation for material actions
- model/prompt versioning where applicable
- sensitive-data protection
- full auditability of AI-assisted actions

**AI should reduce cognitive load, not transfer accountability to a model.**
